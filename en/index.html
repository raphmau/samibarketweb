HOW TO PLAY

Draw a line.
Shrink the field.

  1. 01

    Watch the flock.

    The sheep are on the move. Find an opening and plan your cut.

  2. 02

    Time your swipe.

    Draw a straight line across the field. Keep every sheep clear of your line!

  3. 03

    Clear some space.

    The empty section disappears. Shrink the field to reach the target, then try to do even better.

THE WORLDS OF SLICED TRIP

Sami’s
journey.

From the USA to Peru by way of Mexico: three worlds and 96 levels, with new field shapes to figure out.

On the map, each tree stump shows the shape of a level. Work out your cuts, then come back for the time and cut-count medals.
