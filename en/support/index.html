Support

Sami Barket: Sliced Trip · iPhone and iPad

Contact us

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to us at support@samibarket.com.

To help us answer you, please include if you can:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version;
  • the level concerned and what happened;
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

Purchases and refunds

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

You can change your choices in the game: Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. For details, see the privacy policy.
