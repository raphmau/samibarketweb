Privacy policy

Sami Barket - Sliced Trip · iPhone and iPad · Effective November 12, 2026

1. Who we are

The game is published by RAPHMAU Sàrl, Geneva, Switzerland (UID CHE-166.411.825), the data controller. For any question about your data: support@samibarket.com.

2. What the game keeps on your device

Your progress, wool, lives and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no account and no server of ours: we never receive your name, email address, contacts, precise location or photos.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads, including videos you choose to watch for a reward. They are served through the LevelPlay mediation (Unity / ironSource) and the Google AdMob and Unity Ads networks. To work, these partners receive technical data: device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions, performance and crash data, device language and model. No ad is shown until you have won level 1.10 (the tenth level).

  • Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared only if you allow it in iOS's "Allow Tracking" prompt. You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Without your consent, only non-personalised ads are shown.

4. Your consent

The game asks for your choice after you win the first level (1.1), and therefore before any ad: in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland through Google's consent form (UMP), then through iOS's "Allow Tracking" prompt. In the relevant US states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can reopen these choices at any time in the game: Settings > Privacy > Ad choices.

5. Statistics and crashes

Only with your consent, the game sends usage statistics to Unity Analytics, tied to a random identifier rather than your identity, to help us improve it: for example level started, won or lost, play time, wool balance, the choice you make when you run out of lives, product purchased. They contain neither your name nor your email address. Without consent, no statistics are sent. Technical crash reports are handled by Unity Diagnostics.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases (1 hour unlimited, Premium, Unlimited forever) are processed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details: the game only receives the purchase confirmation.

7. What we do not use

The game does not use Unity AdQuality, builds no advertising profile of its own and sells no data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), you may request access to, correction or erasure of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time. As we keep no data on servers of our own, these requests mostly concern our partners: write to support@samibarket.com and we will help you exercise them. Deleting the game erases its save from your device. You may also contact the data protection authority of your country (in Switzerland, the FDPIC).

Our partners may process data outside Switzerland and the EU, notably in the United States, under their own policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Unity Analytics, Diagnostics)
  • Google (AdMob, UMP)
  • Apple (in-app purchases)

9. Family audience

The game is meant for the whole family. It is not in the App Store's Kids category and is not directed specifically at children under 13 within the meaning of the US COPPA.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its effective date.
